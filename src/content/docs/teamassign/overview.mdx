TeamAssign is a comprehensive web application designed to facilitate academic team management, peer evaluation, and survey administration. Developed originally at a major research university, the system has been in continuous operation for over 15 years, serving thousands of instructors and hundreds of thousands of students across hundreds of higher education institutions globally.

This documentation serves as a central repository for institutional knowledge. It is designed to onboard new developers, system administrators, and technical support engineers to a mature, legacy codebase, ensuring the continued stability and evolution of the platform.

## System Purpose

In academic settings, forming effective teams and gathering reliable peer feedback are complex logistical challenges. TeamAssign automates these processes, providing instructors with tools to:

- Create complex surveys to gather student preferences, schedules, and skills.
- Form optimal teams using constraint-based algorithms.
- Administer and grade peer evaluations based on defined rubrics.
- Export actionable data for grading and analysis.

## Technology Stack

TeamAssign is built on a traditional, robust LAMP-style stack, optimized for stability and high throughput under academic workloads (e.g., end-of-semester evaluation spikes).

## Scale and Impact

The platform operates at a significant scale, necessitating careful consideration of database query performance and server resource management.

- **User Base**: Over 5,000 active instructors and 200,000+ student accounts.
- **Institutions**: Adopted by over 300 universities and colleges.
- **Data Volume**: Millions of survey responses and peer evaluation records stored and processed annually.
- **Longevity**: 15+ years of continuous operation, reflecting a proven, highly resilient architecture.

## Key Features

### Constraint-Based Team Formation
The core value proposition of TeamAssign lies in its ability to form teams based on complex, instructor-defined criteria. The platform uses custom algorithms to group students based on schedule compatibility, skill distribution (e.g., ensuring every team has a strong programmer), or demographic diversity.

### Peer Evaluation Engine
Instructors can design detailed peer evaluation rubrics. The system manages the distribution of these evaluations, tracks completion rates, and aggregates the results into comprehensive reports, often applying weightings to identify "free-riders" or exceptional contributors.

### Survey Administration
A flexible survey builder allows for the creation of multiple-choice, free-text, and Likert-scale questions to gather necessary data for team formation or general course feedback.

### Data Interoperability
Given its integration into varied academic ecosystems, TeamAssign heavily supports bulk data operations. Robust CSV import/export capabilities allow instructors to sync rosters from their Learning Management Systems (LMS) and export final grades seamlessly.
