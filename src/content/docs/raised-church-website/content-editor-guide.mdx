This guide explains how you can update the church website's content using the Contentful platform. You do not need any programming knowledge to make changes—everything is done through a simple web interface.

## What is Contentful?

Contentful is a Content Management System (CMS). You can think of it as the online control panel for your website. 

When you add, edit, or delete content in Contentful, those changes automatically update the live church website. The system manages your text, images, and files in one central place.

## Logging in to Contentful

<Steps>

1. Open your web browser and navigate to [contentful.com](https://www.contentful.com).
2. Click **Log in** in the top right corner.
3. Choose **Continue with GitHub** (or your designated login method).
4. Once logged in, you will see the workspace (Space) for the church website.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
If you are logging in for the first time and cannot see any content, ask your website administrator to ensure you have been added to the correct workspace.
</Aside>

## Managing sermons

You can upload weekly sermons, including video links and PDF resources.

<Steps>

1. Navigate to the **Content** tab at the top of the page.
2. Click **Add entry** and select **Sermon**.
3. Fill in the sermon **Title**, **Speaker**, and **Date**.
4. To add the video, find the **YouTube ID** field. Do not paste the full URL. Instead, copy only the ID from the end of the YouTube link (e.g., if the link is `youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ`, enter `dQw4w9WgXcQ`).
5. (Optional) Upload the presentation slides and weekly newsletter as PDF files in their respective fields.
6. Click **Publish** to make the sermon live.

</Steps>

## Managing events and news

You can post announcements and upcoming events.

<Steps>

1. Click **Add entry** and select **Church News**.
2. Set the **Type** to either `announcement` (for general news) or `event` (for activities with a specific time/place).
3. Fill in the **Title** and **Body** text.
4. If it is an event, enter the **Date** and **Location**.
5. (Optional) Provide a **Sign-up URL** (like a Google Form link) and upload a cover image (recommended size: 800×450 pixels).
6. Ensure **Is Active** is set to "Yes".
7. Click **Publish**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
When an event has passed, you don't need to delete it. Just change **Is Active** to "No" and republish. The event will disappear from the website.
</Aside>

## Updating static pages

Some pages have fixed structures but editable text, such as the About, Gathering Times, and Offering pages.

- **About Page:** Search for the "About Page" entry. You can edit the church history paragraphs and the statement of faith. You can also link "Pastor" entries here.
- **Gathering Times:** Search for "Gathering Time" entries to update the weekly schedule shown on the homepage. Edit the Day, Time, and Order.
- **Offering:** Search for the "Offering" entry to update bank transfer details, account numbers, and on-site offering instructions.

## Bilingual content

The website supports both Traditional Chinese and English. When editing an entry, look for the language switcher in the sidebar (usually showing `zh-Hant-TW` and `en-US`). 

Switch to English to fill in the English text, and switch back to Chinese for the Chinese text. Both will be saved and displayed to the user based on their language preference on the site.

## Common mistakes and fixes

- **Changes aren't showing up on the website:** Did you click **Publish**? Clicking "Save" only creates a draft. Also, keep in mind that it may take 2-3 minutes for Vercel to rebuild and deploy the site after you publish changes in Contentful.
- **YouTube video is broken:** Ensure you only entered the video ID (e.g., `dQw4w9WgXcQ`), not the entire `https://...` URL.
- **Deleted something by accident:** Don't panic! Contentful keeps a version history. Open the entry, look for the **Versions** sidebar, and you can roll back to a previous state.

## Requesting new custom pages

If you need a completely new type of page (e.g., a special landing page for a conference) that doesn't fit the existing templates, you will need to request this from your developer. They will need to create a new content model in Contentful and write the corresponding Svelte code to display it.

## Support contact

If you encounter technical issues or need access permissions adjusted, please contact your website administrator or the technical lead for the church.
