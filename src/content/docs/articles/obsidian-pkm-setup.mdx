## Overview

This article documents the design and implementation of a **local-first personal knowledge management (PKM) system** built with Obsidian. It examines the information architecture decisions — taxonomy design, linking strategy, and plugin workflow automation — and demonstrates how these principles transfer directly to building internal documentation systems at scale.
